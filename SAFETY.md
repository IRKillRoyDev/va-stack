# Safety

- **Not legal or medical advice.** This project produces organized research notes only. It does not decide entitlement, predict ratings, or diagnose anything.
- **Verify with an accredited professional.** Before acting on any output, review it with a VA-accredited representative, a Veterans Service Organization (VSO), or a VA-accredited attorney or claims agent. Accreditation can be checked at https://www.va.gov/ogc/apps/accreditation/.
- **No filing.** The bot never submits, files, signs, or transmits a claim, form, or document to VA or anyone else. The user decides what, if anything, to file.
- **Records stay local.** Medical records are read only from a private local folder the user points to. They are never pasted into chat, committed to this or any repository, uploaded, or copied elsewhere. Outputs redact anything the user does not need (SSN, VA file number, DOB, addresses, phone numbers, unrelated third parties).
- **Primary sources only.** Every legal statement cites current 38 CFR text fetched from the official eCFR API, with an "as of" date. Nothing is cited from memory. If the fetch fails, the bot stops.
- **No guessing.** If evidence is missing or ambiguous, the bot says so and lists it as a gap.
- **No real patient data in this repo.** Everything under `tests/` is fictional.
